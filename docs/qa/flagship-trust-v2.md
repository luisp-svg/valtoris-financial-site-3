# Flagship trust revision 2

New Family and Business forms submit `flagshipVersion: 2`. Earlier version-one
questionnaires retain their labels and required answers; the original version
remains valid for saved reports. No database migration is needed.

Flagship overall grades now require at least five substantive planning answers
and 80% of applicable planning checks. Unknown and private answers remain
unscored; not-applicable checks are excluded. Sparse answers produce no overall
numeric score or letter. Category grades require 80% information coverage in
that category. Reports display information completeness next to preparation.
This display correction also protects reopened version-one reports; historical
stored scores are not rewritten. A grade still measures self-reported planning
preparation, not verified adequacy, wealth or product suitability.

Revision-two questions use a 12-month/relevant-change review standard. Family
compensation distinguishes salary, variable pay, equity, business and investment
income, with an equity coordination follow-up only when relevant. Business
questions address 30-day absence authority, household dependence on owner pay,
financial statements and ownership valuation/funding. Context observations
surface changes and unresolved authority alongside the three review priorities.

Phone is optional only for revision-two flagships. Invalid supplied numbers are
rejected, optional SMS consent still requires a valid number, and older forms
keep their existing validation. Booking uses the saved email and identity.

A shared bilingual offer appears before the form, at saving and at booking:
30 minutes with Luis Perez, two leading priorities, professional coordination
and agreed next steps. It states no obligation to buy, costs and compensation
explained before implementation, and tax/legal boundaries. No fees or credentials
have been invented. The existing real-calendar routing is unchanged.

Staging must verify sparse-grade Family, substantive-grade Business, compensation
branching, no-employee/sole-owner paths, optional-phone persistence and actual
silent-calendar booking, English/Spanish copy, and a narrow mobile viewport
before production publication. Exact evidence is recorded with the release.
