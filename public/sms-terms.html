SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Program description

The Valtoris Financial LLC Customer Care SMS program provides informational messages about inquiries you initiate, appointment scheduling and reminders, requested service updates, and customer support. Messages may come from 737-374-7333 or 949-243-7484 after the applicable sender registration is approved. These terms cover customer care and informational messages, not promotional or marketing campaigns.

Consent and eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and the subscriber or authorized user of the phone number you provide. You opt in by selecting the separate, optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox in our contact message form and submitting it. Providing a phone number without selecting SMS consent does not authorize texts. Messages may use automated technology. Consent is not a condition of purchase or receiving services. You may contact us by email or phone without opting in to SMS.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your inquiries, appointments, and requested services. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Contact your mobile provider for plan details.

How to opt out

Text STOP to the sending number to unsubscribe. You may receive a final message confirming your opt-out. For assistance stopping messages across both Valtoris numbers, contact us using the details below. Do not send further sensitive information in a text message.

Help and customer support

Text HELP to the sending number for help, email luisp@valtorisfinancial.com, or call 737-374-7333. You may also call 949-243-7484.

Delivery and carrier liability

Delivery is subject to mobile network availability and your device and carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We do not guarantee delivery or response times. Do not use this messaging program for emergencies.

Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and retain information. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Providers that operate and deliver our messaging service may process this information solely to provide that service.

Changes and contact information

We may update these terms and publish the revised effective date here. New message purposes require appropriate consent. Questions may be sent to Valtoris Financial LLC, 405 Pheasant Ridge Circle, Round Rock, TX 78665, or luisp@valtorisfinancial.com.
